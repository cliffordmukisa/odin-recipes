Chicken Stew

Description

Chicken stew is a comforting, hearty dish featuring tender chicken pieces, root vegetables, and a savory, thick broth

Ingredients

  • Boneless or bone-in chicken thighs
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Spices

Steps

  1. Brown the chicken in a heavy pot or Dutch oven
  2. Sauté the aromatics and vegetables
  3. Stir in flour to create a roux, pour in chicken stock and wine, return the chicken and potatoes, and simmer or bake until tender.
